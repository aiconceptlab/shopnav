I turned a store map into a shopping guide. 🛠️

Meet ShopNav.

Search “M8 bolts”. See the shelf. Follow a walkable route.
Add a paint brush and wall plugs, then plan a route through the shelf stops and finish at checkout.

For the store owner: edit shelves, paths and product locations in a draft, check it, then publish. Customers keep seeing the last published map while you work.

The free POC includes a hardware store with 36 products, 12 shelves and QR checkpoints. No paid API keys.

The useful part is the workflow. Routing is deterministic, not AI, and the start point is manually confirmed — no indoor GPS or live customer tracking. Stock is manually maintained too.
